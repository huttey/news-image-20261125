## The image

A one-page newspaper front — **THE WORLD, Wednesday 25 November 2026** — summing up
the day's world news in one picture. 37 stories, every one of them first reported on
25 November 2026: the Asia sell-off as the AI rally fizzled and fresh US tariff threats
landed (Nikkei -2.8%, Kospi -5.3%, Taiwan -5.1%), Shenzhou-24 launching with the first
foreign astronaut to reach Tiangong, the Shanghai loyalty pledge after the Politburo
probe, Trump's float of a pardon for Nvidia chief Jensen Huang, the leaked FIFA
documents on Infantino's 96-year World Cup plan, the US Army detonating its
bomb-chemical stockpile in Iowa, and 31 more stories in the day-in-brief index.

Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC, The Guardian, Economic Times, TechCrunch, Variety,
Mongabay, NWS, Sportradar.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (standard library only, no network).
`draw.py` regenerates `entry.png` from the story list with PIL — it is build tooling,
kept in the repo for transparency.
